💡 Cool Fact

Why Do Autumn Leaves Turn Red?

Trees stop producing green chlorophyll, revealing the yellow pigments that were hidden all summer long!

💡 Science Detail

The Role of Anthocyanin

While yellows are always present in leaves, red pigments (anthocyanins) are produced only in autumn when trapped sugars react with sunlight!
