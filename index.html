This Week's Story

What a whirlwind of a week it has been! Between Christine’s departure for Korea and tackling many work-related tasks—including working toward the Google Generative AI Professional Certificate—as well as taking care of household responsibilities such as arranging a deck renovation contract before winter arrives, preparing daily meals, doing laundry, watering the plants, and driving the Camry, I barely had time to slow down. The weather is finally cooling down, which allows me to spend more time outside and go running. 

The highlight of this week was adjusting to life on my own. I have spent most of my life living with my spouse, but for the next two and a half months, I need to find a way to live well by myself. Having things to focus on—such as work, church, running, and household responsibilities—certainly helps. I also decided to create this website to share my life with my family. This is something new for me, but I enjoy doing it, whether you like it or not! 

The biggest challenge this week was having trouble sleeping after Christine left for Korea. Of course, I have struggled with sleep issues for the past several months, so this is not entirely new to me, although I still do not like it at all. I have not yet figured out how to fall asleep more quickly and sleep better. I believe that God is the one who can ultimately help me overcome these mental struggles. Another ongoing challenge is managing everything by myself. There are so many things competing for every minute of my day, both at work and at home. I need to standardize and simplify my daily routine. And, of course, I already miss Christine a lot. 

I plan to share my story every week, and I hope you find something interesting in my weekly updates. You are certainly welcome to share your own stories with us as well. I can create a website for you—it is much easier now because of generative AI. In any case, just let me know!
